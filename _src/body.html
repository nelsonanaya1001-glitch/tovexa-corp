Common questions

How do I open a wholesale account?

Submit a resale certificate and business license with the catalog request form. Verification typically takes 24–48 hours, and there is no cost to apply.

What are your minimum order quantities?

MOQs are set per category, most starting between 50 and 100 units. Pallet-priced lines are quoted per pallet. Volume tiers apply automatically as order size grows.

Are your products authentic and brand-authorized?

Yes. Everything is sourced brand-direct or through authorized distribution. Invoices and authenticity documentation are available for any lot on request.

Do you support drop-shipping to end customers?

Yes, for approved accounts, with blind packing slips and direct integrations for Shopify, WooCommerce, and Amazon.

How fast do orders ship?

Standard orders dispatch within 24 hours from the nearest regional warehouse. Most of the continental US is covered by two-day ground.

What if something arrives damaged or wrong?

Open an RMA from your account dashboard. Damaged or incorrect stock is resolved within five business days with no restocking fee.
